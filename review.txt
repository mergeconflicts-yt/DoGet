2. High
1. v Steps added later in the day never count, in Engagement.swift:39-68.
• The Evaluator sets lastEvaluatedKey to today. Every later refresh then starts at tomorrow, so Health is never reread that day.
• Step 3 evaluates only from that run's in-memory cache. Every Health day reads as 0 on later refreshes.
• Result: a 10k-steps day that crosses the goal at 18:00 never becomes a win, with no Day won and no ledger entry.
• Fix: always re-fetch from yesterday (or the last evaluated day, whichever is earlier) through today, and build day results from stored MetricDay rows plus manual logs (DATABASE §5).
2. Day won and the celebration only fire for per-win rewards (Engagement.swift:100-137). End, jar, item-end and One time challenges never show Day won or the celebration. "Done" is never found by comparing old and new snapshots.
3. v A rings target of 3 can never be won for most users (ActivitySources.swift:99). Move is checked with appleMoveTime, not activeEnergyBurned against its goal.
4. V Level up isn't implemented at all: no Derive function, no Celebration button, no tests (A28-A31, R06, R09). TASK.md wrongly marks it done.
5. Rewards can be claimed twice (MomentsViews.swift:121). "See your trophy" offers "Claim reward" again for an already-claimed challenge, and a double tap writes duplicate final entries.
6. V A No end item with a value can never be claimed (ChallengePageView.swift:431). The WORTH footer branch comes before the "I'm done • claim" branch.
7. Nudges can fire on the wrong day (Engagement.swift: 188). A refresh after 20:00 schedules today's nudge for tomorrow 20:00 with today's numbers, and it isn't removed when tomorrow is won. Nudges for scrapped challenges are never removed.
8. Dynamic Type is unsupported. Every font is a fixed size, and there's no @ScaledMetric (AGENTS §3.8).
9. Manual mode has no Log today button. A small pill replaces the large black button described in SPEC $7.9.

3. Medium: domain and data
• V Wrong reward phrases (Derive.swift: 32-50):
• Trip "Iceland" gives "an trip to Iceland". Food gives "a Pizza" (should be "a treat: Pizza"). Other gives "a Concert" (should be "Concert").
• Money phrases say "a win" where COPY says "each time".
• V Item savings can exceed the price (Derive.swift: 17). A 2100 item over 40 wins saves 7117 after 39 wins, and NEXT WIN shows -317. Cap the amount at the value.
• V One time ignores today in the engine (Engine.swift:112). The app is saved by Mapping passing a best that includes
today, but the A13 test is hollow because it passes best = 21.1 directly.
• Validation order and text are off (Derive.swift:84-100). The reward should be checked second, and "Name your reward", "Rings target is 1 to 3" and "Set wins and days" aren't in COPY.
• Yesterday turns final before 03:00 (Mapping.swift:26). Backfilled older days are never marked final.
• Item-saving amounts are wrong when several days are backfilled at once (Engagement.swift:108). They use the current win count, not that day's.
• Per-win money keeps being added after a challenge is done or past its window (Engagement.swift:100).
• Past days that become wins within the grace period never show Day won. Out of reach and day won notifications are never sent.
• The 2-second debounce drops calls instead of merging them. A Log today save right after a foreground refresh isn't evaluated.
• The background refresh task doesn't run the Evaluator. HealthKit observers are registered in onAppear (should be App init), and HealthKit's completion is called before the Evaluator runs.
• Sleep uses an overlap window instead of an end-time window, so samples can count in two days.
• The dedupe pass skips ManualLog and ChallengeRecord.
• Nudge money ignores the currency.
• The iCloud backup flag is stored in AppSettings, where DATABASE 82 says UserDefaults.
• CreateDraft.swift (in Domain) imports Observation, where Domain should use Foundation only.
