Reward Ideas for Reaching a Weight Goal

The classic mistake is rewarding weight loss with food. The fix: reward the behaviours that move weight — daily steps, workouts, sleep — with prizes that make the next month easier or more fun.

How to structure it in wonday

wonday tracks activity, not kilograms — which is actually the healthier deal. Set an activity challenge (10,000 steps on 20 of 30 days, or 12 workouts this month) and attach the weight-goal prize to that. You control the inputs; the scale follows.

Reward ideas that work

  • Gear: running shoes, a good bottle, a gym bag, a dumbbell set.
  • Recovery: a massage, a spa day, an afternoon nap, guilt-free.
  • Experiences: a weekend trip, concert tickets, a day off.
  • Wardrobe: one piece that fits the new you — earned, not bought early.
  • Milestone money: ₹1,000 per won week into a “goal fund” jar.

Ideas to skip

  • Food binges as prizes — they undo the behaviour you’re rewarding.
  • Prizes so big they scare you into quitting (keep the first prize small).
  • All-or-nothing rules — use 20-of-30 or a jar so one bad week can’t kill the plan.

Remember the deal: you pay for your own rewards, wonday only keeps the score. That keeps every prize honest — and earned.
