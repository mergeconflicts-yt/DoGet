Help & FAQ

Everything about the iPhone app that rewards you for walking, moving and sleeping well.

What is wonday?

wonday is an iPhone app that rewards you for walking and other healthy habits. Set a daily goal, choose your own reward, and Apple Health counts for you. Win enough days and the reward is yours.

Does wonday pay me money?

No. You pay for your own rewards — a trip, shoes, a treat, anything you choose. wonday only keeps the score: it tracks your wins and tells you when a reward is earned.

Does wonday need an Apple Watch?

No. Your iPhone already counts steps through Apple Health, so you can start today with the phone in your pocket. An Apple Watch can improve accuracy for workouts and rings, but it is optional.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No sign-up, no account, no server. Everything works on your iPhone, including offline.

Is my health data private?

Yes. wonday reads Apple Health data read-only to keep score. It never writes to Apple Health, and health data never leaves your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

What goals can I set?

Steps, activity rings, workouts or exercise minutes, and sleep — anything Apple Health already tracks. No logging: your day fills up on its own.

What happens if I miss a day?

That’s fine. Pick a flexible rule (for example 20 wins of 30 days), keep a streak, or fill a savings jar with every win.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later, using Apple Health.

Is wonday free?

Yes, wonday is free to download on the App Store.

How do I delete my data?

Your data lives only on your iPhone. Delete a challenge inside the app to remove it, or delete the app to remove everything.
