wonday / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Short version: no sign-up, no account, no server. Your goals, rewards and health data stay on your iPhone.

Last updated: October 2026

Data we don’t collect

wonday has no accounts and no server, so there is nothing to send us. We collect no name, no email, no location, no analytics and no advertising identifiers. The marketing site is static and sets no cookies.

On your iPhone

Your challenges, wins and reward notes are stored only on your device. There is no cloud backup — not even iCloud. Deleting the app deletes everything, so your data never sits on any server, ours or anyone else’s.

Apple Health

wonday reads steps, activity rings, workouts and sleep from Apple Health, only with your permission, only to keep score. Access is read-only: wonday never writes to Apple Health, and health values never leave your iPhone.

No tracking, no ads

No third-party analytics, crash reporters, ad SDKs or cross-app tracking in v1. The app works fully offline and makes no network connections of its own.

Children

Because the app collects nothing and holds no accounts, there is no children’s data to protect. Rewards are self-funded by the user; the app never moves money.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app update and is posted here.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us through the support link on the App Store listing.
