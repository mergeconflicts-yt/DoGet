How to Reward Yourself for Walking 10,000 Steps

10,000 steps a day works — until motivation fades in week two. A reward you chose yourself, tied to days you actually win, is what keeps you walking. Here is the setup that works.

1. Pick a reward worth walking for

Choose one concrete thing: ₹10,000 towards a trip, running shoes, a spa day, pizza night with friends. Write it down as the prize for your challenge. You pay for it yourself — wonday only keeps the score, which is exactly why the deal feels real.

2. Set a rule with room to breathe

Don’t demand perfection. A flexible rule like walk 10,000 steps on 20 of 30 days survives rainy days, travel and bad weeks. Miss a day? That’s fine — you’re still on track.

3. Let your iPhone count

Connect wonday to Apple Health once. Your iPhone already counts steps, so every day fills up on its own — no logging, no Apple Watch required. At 10,000 steps the day is won.

4. Claim your wins

Hit 20 wins and the reward is earned. wonday shows you exactly where you stand — 12 of 20 wins, 8 to go — so the finish line pulls you forward instead of pressuring you.

Reward ideas that work

  • Money per win: ₹500 into a jar for every 10,000-step day.
  • A trip you book only if you finish the month.
  • Gear that makes the next month easier: shoes, a bottle, a pass.
  • Something purely fun: a film night, a favourite meal, a day off.
