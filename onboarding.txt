The name
The app is Wonday. Inside the app, always write it lowercase "wonday", with the "o" drawn as a progress ring, ¼ full (wordmark A in prototype/wonday-brand.html). The ring takes the color of the text around it, with a faint track behind it.
VoiceOver reads "Wonday". System text (notifications, Home Screen name) uses plain "Wonday".
Overall feel
• Bold color, big type, one idea per screen. Playful, but quick.
• Screens 0, 1 (after fill), 3 and 4 are orange. Screen 2 (Health) is white.
• Moving between screens: the new screen slides in from the right, the old one slides a little to the left and fades. Back does the reverse. About half a second, soft ease-out.
• Screens 1-3 have a small header: back arrow and a 3-part progress bar (the current part fills in with a short slide). No Skip in v1.
• Celebrations use confetti, a short phone shake, and a success haptic.
• With Reduce Motion on: no loops, no confetti, no shake. Things appear in their final state.
Screen 0 - Welcome
Goal: explain the idea in 3 seconds.
What is on screen (top to bottom): the wonday logo, a tilted demo card, a small dark reward chip, the headline, three lines of text, the Get started button, and three small chips.
Animations
1. Logo: the ring "o" draws itself once, from empty to ¾ full, just after the screen opens.
2. Background: a few large, soft, see-through circles float slowly up and down. Small white bits drift upward and fade. This is calm, background motion only.
3. Demo card (the main show). A white card, tilted a little, gently bobbing up and down. It plays a loop, about every 3.5 seconds:
• The card starts empty. It shows a goal pill (for example STEPS), "TODAY", the number 0 and "of 10,000 steps".
• Color fills the card from the bottom up, with a small moving wave on the top edge. The number climbs from 0 to the target at the same steady pace, about 2 seconds.
• When the fill passes the text, the text turns white so it stays readable.
• At full, a "wonday " stamp lands on the card (it pops in from big to normal size, slightly rotated, like a rubber stamp).
• A moment later, the dark reward chip springs up from below the card: "YOUR REWARD • 210,000".
• Then the next example starts. The whole screen changes color to the new goal's color, and the loop repeats.
• Examples in order: Steps → z10,000 (orange), Rings → A trip to Taiwan (pink), Exercise → New headphones (green),
Run → New running shoes (blue), Calories → Pizza night (teal), Sleep → A spa day (violet).

Text
• Headline (always 3 lines): Reward / yourself / for moving.
• Under it: "Set a health goal. Choose your reward. Win the day." then in bold: We only keep the score. / You pay for your rewards.
• Button: Get started (white, orange text).
• Chips under the button: v No sign-up • v No account • v No server.
Screen 1 - Walk to unlock
Goal: let the user fee/ the app. Their effort fills the screen and unlocks a reward.
Start state: light orange (tint) background. Centered title Walk 10,000 steps, sub "to unlock your reward." A very big 0 with
"steps" under it. At the bottom, a big round dark button with a footprints icon and the label Hold to walk. A soft ring pulses outward from the button again and again, to invite a press.
While holding
• The button shrinks slightly (pressed feel) and the pulse stops.
• Orange color rises from the bottom of the whole screen, with a moving wave on top.
• The number counts up with the fill. Holding for about 2.3 seconds in total reaches 10,000.
• If the user lets go, the fill and number stop where they are (they do not go back down). Holding again continues.
• As the fill rises, things it covers turn white: first the button (white circle, orange icon) and its label, then the big number, then at the very top the title, header and status bar.
At 10,000 (unlock), in this order
1. The button and the number fade away. Success haptic, a short phone shake, and a confetti burst. The screen is now fully orange.
2. A white reward card springs up into the middle: a gift icon, "REWARD UNLOCKED", and an amount that counts up from
2O to 210,000 in about a second.
3. The Next button fades in at the bottom.
4. Just after, two rows slide up under the card:
• v We only keep the score.
• E You pay for your rewards.
5. The gift icon gives a small wiggle every few seconds.

Rules
• This is a demo only. Do not read Health. Do not save a win or a challenge.
• There is no Skip: the only way forward is holding to unlock, or Back.
• VoiceOver: add an action that completes the walk at once, for people who cannot press and hold.
Screen 2 - Apple Health
White screen. A red Health icon at the top with a gentle heartbeat animation. Title Connect Apple Health, and a short line: wonday only reads data, never writes, uploads or shares it.
A list shows what we read: Steps (marked YOUR GOAL), Activity rings, Exercise minutes, Workouts and distance, Active calories, Sleep. The switches are for display only.
• Connect Apple Health → shows the iS Health permission sheet. When it closes, the button turns green: "Connected
"for a moment, then go to the next screen. Never show an error, because iOS does not tell us if the user said no.
• Not now, I'll log manually → turn on manual mode, show a short toast ("OK. Tap "Log today" to add your activity."), then go next.
Screen 3 - Notifications
Orange screen. At the top, a lock screen preview (time 8:00, date). Two sample notifications drop in one after the other, with a small bounce:
1. "4 hours left • 2,158 steps to make today a wonday."
2. "That's a wonday! / • 10 of 20 done. 10,000 is getting closer."
They clear and drop in again every few seconds.
Title: A nudge, / not spam. Sub: "Only when a win is close, or when you win."
• Allow notifications → shows the iOS prompt. Then the button says "Allowed " for a moment, then go next.
• Not now → go next. Ask again later, after the first real win.

Screen 4 - You're set
Orange screen, no header. The animation plays in sequence:
1. A big white ring draws itself around a circle.
2. A white disc pops into the center.
3. An orange check mark draws itself on the disc.
4. The title You're set. and the sub "Start with this challenge, or make your own." are shown.
5. A white card slides up: "YOUR FIRST CHALLENGE • I'll walk 10,000 steps a day on 20 of 30 days to win 210,000 for each wonday." (Challenges can't be edited once created, so the card makes no edit promise.)
6. Confetti bursts.
• Start this challenge → create the real challenge (steps 10,000, 20 of 30 days, 710,000 each win, named like "October 10K"), open its page, and show the toast "Challenge started. Go make it a wonday!"
• Make my own → open a fresh Create with nothing pre-selected; the user picks the activity and goal.

Must be true
• The user clearly understands: we only keep the score, you pay for your rewards (screens 0 and 1).
• Every in-app "wonday" uses the name style.
• Onboarding shows once; it can be replayed from Settings.
• No error if Health access is refused.
• Reduce Motion: no loops, no confetti, no shake.
