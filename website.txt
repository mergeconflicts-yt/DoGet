Purpose
A one-page site that sells the app in a few seconds and sends people to the App Store. The two messages that must be clear everywhere: We only keep the score. You pay for your rewards. Also: No sign-up • No account • No server.
Overall look
• Same energy as the app: bold full-screen color, very big heavy type, playful motion.
• The page is 6 full-screen color blocks. Each block has one big line and one live phone that shows the app moving.
• Text and phone swap sides from block to block (left/right).
• Soft, see-through circles float slowly in the background of each block.
• When a block scrolls into view, its text slides up and the phone rises into place.
• The app name is always lowercase wonday with the ring "o" (same as the app).
• The top bar (logo, links, "Get the app") stays fixed. It takes the color of the block under it, slightly see-through.
• Works on phones: blocks stack (text on top, phone below).
• With Reduce Motion on: no loops, no confetti.

Block 1 - Hero (orange)
• Line: "Make every day a wonday."
• *Under it: "Set a health goal. Choose your reward. Win the day." and in bold "We only keep the score. You pay for your rewards."
• Buttons: "Download for iPhone" (white) and "Try it here " (text link to block 2).
• Chips: v No sign-up • V No account • V No server.
• Phone: a real-looking challenge page (pill, name, rule, day bars, big win count).
• The screen fills with color from the bottom, with a moving wave on top.
• Day bars turn on one by one, and the win count counts up.
• At the end a "wonday " stamp lands and a small confetti burst pops.
• Every ~4.5 seconds it switches to another challenge (steps, rings, sleep, run).
The whole hero changes color to match.
• Floating cards next to the phone bob gently: "DAY WON +z10,000" and "REWARD • Trip to Taiwan".


Block 2 - Try it (blue)
• Line: "Walk. Watch it fill."
• Text: "Hold the button on the phone. At 10,000 steps, the reward is yours." and in bold "We only keep the score. You pay for your rewards."
• Phone (interactive): "Walk 10,000 steps", a step counter at 0 and a round Hold to walk button with a pulsing ring.
• While the visitor holds, the phone screen fills with blue and the counter goes up.
Letting go pauses it.
• Text turns white as the fill covers it.
• At 10,000: confetti, a small shake, and a white "REWARD UNLOCKED 710,000" card pops up and counts up.
• A "Walk again S" link appears to reset.

Block 3 - Your deal (pink)
• Line: "You choose the reward."
• Text: "Money, a trip, a treat, or the thing you want. Write the whole deal in one line!" and in bold "You pay for it. We only keep the score."
• Phone: the "New challenge" screen. The sentence writes itself: "'I [walk 10,000 steps] [on 20 of 30 days] to win [R10,000 for each win]."
• Each blank starts dashed and pops into a filled pill, one after the other.
• Then the "Start challenge" button lights up.
• It loops through other deals (rings → trip to Taiwan, run → running shoes, sleep → spa day, calories → pizza night).

Block 4 - Your pace (green)
• Line: "Miss a day? That's fine."
• Text: "Win 20 of 30 days, keep a streak, or fill a savings jar. Pick the rule that fits your life."
• Phone: a challenge page that cycles through 3 rules:
• Flexible — some red missed days, and a badge "3 missed • still on track".
• Streak — all bars in a row, "19 days in a row".
• Savings jar — money count-up "786,000 of $2,00,000".
• Each time, the fill rises, the bars turn on, and the number counts up.

Block 5 - Just you (dark)
• Line: "Just you."
• Text: "No sign-up. No account. No server. Your goals, rewards and health data stay on your iPhone."
• Phone: a white screen with an orange lock. The lock snaps shut, then four rows slide in one by one: No sign-up, No account, No server (Never sent to us), Read only (We never write to Apple Health). Loops slowly.

Block 6 - Final (orange)
• Line (huge, centered): "Make every day a wonday."
• Text: "Set the goal. Choose the reward. Win the day." + the bold two-line promise.
• Button: "Download for iPhone" (dark).
• Chips: No sign-up • No account • No server.
• Footer: wonday logo, links (Privacy, Back to top), "© 2026 wonday. Made for people who move."

Rules
• Keep it to these 6 blocks. No feature grids, tickers, testimonials or FAQ lists (they were tried and removed).
• Every phone must look like the real app, not like an icon or illustration.
• Do not imply that we pay rewards or handle money.
• The Download buttons link to the App Store page when it exists.
• No tracking scripts or cookies (matches the "No server" promise). A static site is enough
